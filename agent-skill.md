# BangkokBangkok.NET — public article discovery

## Scope
This document is a navigation and citation guide to a public content website.
It does not provide an API, automated actions, account access, or a remote AI assistant.

## Starting point
- Site homepage: https://www.bangkokbangkok.net/
- Primary language: Thai
- Main subjects: business, marketing, technology, AI and international news

## Procedure
1. Open the homepage and identify the most relevant article title and URL.
2. Open the original article page to read its content. Do not rely only on homepage excerpts.
3. When summarizing, distinguish original reporting, editorial views, and quoted claims.
4. Provide a direct URL to each source article. Do not invent publication dates, authors or facts not present on the page.
5. If an article is unavailable, report that rather than treating the homepage excerpt as a complete article.

## Examples of public article pages
- AI Search training: https://www.bangkokbangkok.net/upskill-content-team-ai-search.html
- AI marketing and predictive analytics: https://www.bangkokbangkok.net/predictive-analytics-ai-marketing-champ.html
- Customer experience management: https://www.bangkokbangkok.net/customer-experience-management-tips-ai-marketing.html

## Safety and operation
Use only public, read-only browsing. No login or elevated permissions are provided.
This guide does not authorize transactions, form submissions or changes to the website.
